What I Learned

My Internship Experience

During my internship at Furutani Industries Co., Ltd., I worked on an Excel VBA-based customer management system. This was my first practical experience in system development and VBA programming.

Through this project, I learned how to work with UserForms, manage worksheet data and develop customer search functions. I also learned the importance of understanding requirements, asking questions, working carefully and taking responsibility for completing tasks.

This experience helped me understand the role of system engineering in solving business problems through technology and motivated me to further develop my skills in system development and software engineering.
